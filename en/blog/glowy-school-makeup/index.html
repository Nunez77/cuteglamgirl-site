Glowy 7-Minute Makeup Look for School

Makeup·3 min read

School makeup has one golden rule: look like you are wearing nothing, but glowing. You can pull off this look in exactly 7 minutes.

Minute 1-2: BB Cream

For teens, a BB cream beats a heavy foundation. It evens out your skin tone without looking like makeup. Apply with your fingers or a damp sponge and blend well toward your neck.

Pro tipIf you have a few breakouts, cover them with a little concealer before the BB cream. Dab it on with tiny taps, never rub.

Minute 3: Brows

Your brows frame your whole face. Define their natural shape with a pencil in your hair color. Use short strokes that mimic the hairs. Blend at the end with the pencil brush.

Minute 4-5: Highlighter

A touch of highlighter on your brow bone, inner corners, the tip of your nose and your cupid bow. That is all you need for the glowy effect. No highlighter? A champagne eyeshadow works just as well.

Minute 6: Mascara

One coat on your upper lashes opens your eyes instantly. Apply in a zigzag from the root to the tips. One coat is enough for the natural look.

Minute 7: Lip gloss

A clear gloss or a nude lip finishes the look. Dewy, natural, cute. Done!
