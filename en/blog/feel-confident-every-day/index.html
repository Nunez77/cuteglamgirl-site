How to Feel Glam and Confident Every Day

Wellness·3 min read

Confidence is not something you are born with, it is something you build. And there are concrete things you can do today to start.

The myth of the perfect girl

None of the girls you admire feel perfect on the inside. The confidence you see online is, most of the time, a performance. We all have bad days and insecurities. That is completely normal.

Remember thisFeeling good about yourself does not mean liking yourself 100% of the time. It means treating yourself with kindness even when you are not having your best day.

The power of taking care of yourself

It is not about expensive makeup. It is about making the effort to care for yourself. Showering, doing your hair, wearing clothes you feel comfortable in, these acts tell your brain that you matter.

Talk to yourself like you talk to your best friend

If your friend said "I look awful today", you would never say "yeah, you are right". You would say something kind. Do the same for yourself.

Your power outfit

We all have that one piece that makes us feel unstoppable. Find it and wear it on the days you need it most: presentations, hard days, nervous situations.

Move your body

Exercise releases endorphins that lift your mood in minutes. Dancing in your room, going for a walk, stretching for 10 minutes. Your body and your mind will thank you.

One thing every day

Confidence is built on evidence. Every time you do something scary, you prove to your brain that you can. Do one small thing that makes you nervous each day. Over time, you will be surprised how far you came.
