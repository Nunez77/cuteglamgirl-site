What's Actually Worth Learning as a Teenager

Confidence & growth·3 min read

There's an advantage we have at our age that nobody explains to us: our brain will never learn this fast again. What costs adults years and a lot of money, we soak up almost without noticing.

The problem is it feels like we have all the time in the world, so we leave it for later. But "later" is exactly when it gets hard.

It's not about school

Heads up: this isn't about getting straight A's. It's about the things that are best learned young and stay with us for life:

A language. At our age the accent sticks almost on its own. As adults it costs triple and never sounds the same.

Something with your hands or body. An instrument, a sport, dancing, drawing. The coordination you train now stays forever.

Reading for fun. Not for homework. Reading whatever we feel like makes us think better, write better, and even understand people better.

The trick isn't talent, it's consistency

You don't have to be a genius. The girl who practices 20 minutes almost every day beats, in the long run, the "talented" one who keeps quitting. The hard part isn't starting, it's not giving up at the first bump. And that can be trained too.

🔮 Message from the future

so many adults say "I wish I'd kept it up while it was easy". And guess who's standing right in that easy moment right now.

Plant today what you'll show off tomorrow

Picture yourself a few years from now speaking another language, playing something, moving with confidence on a court or a stage. All of that starts with one tiny decision today: pick one thing and give it a little time each day.
