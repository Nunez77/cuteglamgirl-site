15 Cute Outfits With Clothes You Already Have

Fashion·3 min read

The secret of girls who always look put together is not owning a ton of clothes, it is knowing how to mix what they already have. Here are 15 combos that work with basics.

The basics you need

Dark jeans, light jeans, a white tee, a black tee, a neutral hoodie, white sneakers and one colorful piece. With that you can build any look on this list.

For school

  • 1. Classic cool: Dark jeans + white tee + white sneakers + thin chain.
  • 2. Cozy chic: Beige hoodie + skinny jeans + white sneakers + small bag.
  • 3. Monochrome: Everything in one shade. It looks sophisticated and it is so easy.
  • 4. Pop of color: Neutral outfit + one bright accessory (earrings, backpack, scrunchie).
  • 5. Easy layering: Tee + open plaid shirt on top + jeans. Tie the shirt at your waist.
Golden ruleIf you do not know what to wear, pick two colors max and one accessory. Less is more.

For hanging out with friends

  • 6. Chic casual: White jeans + colorful top + sandals.
  • 7. Sporty glam: Leggings + crop top + denim jacket + sneakers.
  • 8. Vintage vibes: Mom jeans + tee tucked in + thin belt + platform sneakers.
  • 9. Coquette: Pleated skirt + bow blouse + ballet flats.
  • 10. Street style: Cargo pants + crop hoodie + chunky sneakers.

For special events

  • 11. Simple elegant: Black dress pants + simple blouse + loafers.
  • 12. Floral forever: Floral dress + denim jacket + white sneakers.
  • 13. All black glam: All black with different textures (denim + satin top + booties).
  • 14. Pastel dream: Two pastels close on the color wheel (lilac + pink, mint + baby blue).
  • 15. Denim on denim: Jeans + a denim jacket in a different shade.
